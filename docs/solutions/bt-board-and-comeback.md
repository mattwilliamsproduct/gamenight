# Board hug, phone nav, and per-game comebacks

A lead-toast proof has to come from a submitted round. Injecting "Ann & Bo" onto a Megan & Matt / Cat & Mike board reads as a wrong name. Enter the round so the trailing team passes, then shoot that toast.

Canasta team names stay on one line (`Megan & Matt`) when the cell has room. Do not force `Megan &` / `Matt` onto two lines. A two-team card ends at the last row. The phone shell can still fill the screen; the card does not. Other games keep the stable card width.

Phone nav, top and bottom, says Player Passports and Settings. The 768 row still uses the short Settings mark.

Biggest comeback is ranked inside each game. Explore shows Biggest Wizard comeback at 100 and Biggest Canasta comeback at 180. Raw points do not pick one winner across games. The deficit math is unchanged. Comeback Extra stays off.
