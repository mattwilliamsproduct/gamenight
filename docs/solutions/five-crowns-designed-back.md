# Five Crowns designed back

Matt's photo is the reference for the real Five Crowns back. It is not the picker face.

The Home face is a clean 700×980 SVG raster (`public/assets/cards/five-crowns.svg` → `five-crowns.webp`). Canasta sets the polish: flat color, crisp type, edge to edge, no photo grain. The mark still has to read as Five Crowns: deep purple lattice, lighter-purple gold-edged band, and a sideways gold-and-red FIVE CROWNS logo. The gold frame is crown-shaped: a short FIVE tier and a longer wider CROWNS tier that overhangs the center band. One jeweled gold crown (suit gems, not a row of five) sits in the FIVE field. Lettering is chunky white serif with a gold outline and a thin dark inner line; CROWNS is much larger than FIVE. At 136×190 the words stay readable. The frame spans about 65-70% of card width and about 60% of height. No copyright. No tagline.

Do not retouch, warp, or straighten the photo. Do not run `fit-card-faces.py` on this face; it is already 5:7.

Rook and Beat the Heat defaults are the edge-filled faces. Pads use background only so a crow tail or pepper flame does not repeat at the edge. Heat pads get a short horizontal blend so a wrapped strip does not tile as vertical streaks.
