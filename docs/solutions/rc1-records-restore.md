# Records restore

What broke: Slice 2 preview and the Warm Porch Ledger default still computed the old book, then painted a podium, a 6x2 ledger, five Shame rows, one rivalry line, and thin Past nights. Resume sat on every finished night. Dates sorted as strings, so 7/7 appeared before 7/18.

What fixed it: Records is one destination with Overview, Explore records, and Past nights. Explore restores the main inventory as ledger rows. Night cards carry standings, joint winners, an Assisted badge, and Resume only when the match is incomplete. Win rate always prints its denominator. Head-to-head is who finished ahead in a shared match.

What not to repeat: do not cap Shame at 5. Do not put Resume on a completed night. Do not compare raw points across games. Do not credit a later win to a Life Preserver. Do not use 818 Brick as a primary label.
