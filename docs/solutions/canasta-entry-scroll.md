# Canasta entry scroll

Three teams, or three to six singles, use the same full-size card as a two-team entry. The card list scrolls. The keypad stays a compact column beside it. Two team cards stay side by side at 1024x768, including the largest text setting (root 24px).

A one-card-at-a-time strip would hide the other hands on a wide screen. Shrinking the type so every card fits one screen fails the largest text setting. Scrolling keeps the helper, the checkboxes, and the live total at the two-team size.

An older round is total only. The label is `Older round · total only`. Edit total writes the saved total. It does not invent Basic, Count, Got the 22, Went out, or Cards left. A convert path is not in this release.

A Wizard or 818 cell tap still opens the correction sheet. The headings are Bid, Took, and Points. Each row shows the old points, the new points, and the new running total. A bid or a trick above that round's trick count is rejected as it is typed.
