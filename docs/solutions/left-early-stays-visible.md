# Left early stays on the board

A player who leaves keeps their score on the live scoreboard, Home standings, history, and the scorecard. The place says Left early. They are not 1st, 2nd, or last, and they are not a winner.

Passport Best score skips that partial total. That records exclusion is Matt, 5:25 PM ET: "partial totals never qualify for full-game score records." Keeping the same total off an end-screen celebration is a recommendation, pending Matt. See [ao-provenance.md](ao-provenance.md). If no other counted score exists, Best score is `-`.

The old chip that listed only the names is gone. Last place and head-to-head still use finished games only.
