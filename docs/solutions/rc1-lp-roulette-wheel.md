# Life Preserver roulette wheel

Matt killed outside pills, leader lines, and one thin sliver per rare outcome. The wheel is now a roulette ring.

- One outcome is a unique `(kind, adjustment)`. If game-relative clamping empties the medium band, its 30% folds into small and equal amounts merge. Duke at max-help 20 is −5/−10/−20/0/+5/+10, not a missing medium painted as a weird extra wedge.
- Each outcome splits into several ~2.5% segments. The summed arc of those segments is the table probability. The result still locks before the animation. The pointer lands on a random segment of that outcome, inset from the edges.
- Labels are signed text inside every segment. Rotate them radially when the chord is tight. Never draw a callout.
- The pie fills the ring. Size the modal from leftover viewport so Use Your One Spin stays on screen.

Prod on `main` is the inspiration for in-slice value text and a full-radius pie. Do not copy prod's recovery-load odds or red/green colors.
