# Life Preserver roulette wheel

Matt killed outside pills, leader lines, and one thin sliver per rare outcome. The wheel is now a roulette ring.

- One outcome is a unique `(kind, adjustment)`. If game-relative clamping empties the medium band, its 30% folds into small and equal amounts merge. Duke at max-help 20 is −5/−10/−20/0/+5/+10, not a missing medium painted as a weird extra wedge.
- About 38-40 pockets. Each outcome's pockets share an equal width of `prob / count`. Counts keep those widths within about 25% of the average. Live odds stay 45/30/5/12/8. The closest even-width plan (40 pockets at 18/12/2/5/3 = 45/30/5/12.5/7.5) is documented and not used.
- The result still locks before the animation. A ball rolls the track and settles in a pocket of the locked outcome, inset from the edges. Reduced motion fades the ball into that pocket.
- Labels are signed text in the numbered rim, reading along the wheel. Never draw a callout. No casino red/black.
- The wheel is a bowl: wood rim, frets, turret hub, ball track. Size the modal from leftover viewport so the bowl is the dominant object. Use Your One Spin never scrolls off screen.

Prod on `main` is the inspiration for in-slice value text and a full-radius pie. Do not copy prod's recovery-load odds or red/green colors.
