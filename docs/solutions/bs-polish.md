# Wheel, comeback name, and the Canasta sum

The wheel control belongs on the right of the player cell. The name ends before it. The name fitter has to subtract the badge, or the name paints through "Spin ready". On a phone the badge shrinks and stays in that same spot. Widening the player column hides the hand a wheel followed and squeezes the round cells, so the column keeps its existing width.

A comeback label uses the shelf name from `BPGGames.displayName`. Hand and Foot reads Canasta. A team that was last at halfway keeps both partners on the row.

A Canasta round in progress is one labeled line, such as `Basic 800 + Count 150 = 950 so far`. A blank round says `Not ready` once. Do not print a raw `800 + 150 + 0 + 0 – —` with a second dash under it.

The lead toast stays inside the scorecard. On a phone it sits above the bottom nav and still ends before R3.
