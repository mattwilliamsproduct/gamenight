## Roulette wheel

`roulette-wheel-cc0.jpg` is a public-domain photograph of a real roulette wheel.

- Source: [File:Gambling image.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gambling_image.jpg) on Wikimedia Commons
- Photographer: Stefan Schweihofer / Pixabay
- License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Used as the look target for the Life Preserver wheel: wood bowl, numbered rim, frets, turret, and ball track. Live colors stay teal / gray / coral. Live odds stay 45/30/5/12/8.

Pocket rhythm is 38-40 even slots, like a U.S. wheel, without copying casino red/black or 0/00 labels.
